What Season 1 looks like right now

Keith and Dan are the raw-golf class of the league, averaging 1.57 and 1.71 in gross finish through their appearances. But the handicap competition is telling a very different story: CRANK has turned a 5.0 average gross finish into the best average net finish in the league at 2.71 and already owns three net wins.

That split is basically the whole point. Some weeks the best golfer also wins the tournament. Other weeks the spreadsheet drags everybody back into the fight and somebody has to survive their own version of golf.

  • Keith: leads Season 1 with four gross wins and still owns the most complete all-around game.
  • Dan: has two gross wins and by far the most violence available off the tee.
  • CRANK: owns three net wins while maintaining an active hostility toward greens in regulation.
  • Mashed: remains the chaos ceiling — including the Tobacco Road gross-and-net double.
  • IUDoc92: just detonated Sage Valley at -7 net, turning one good week into the most interesting new Season 1 storyline.
