KeithK75 ran away with the net title at +1 while also posting the only under-par gross round, a -3 demolition powered by 8.05 strokes gained on approach. Behind him, Shinnecock turned the rest of the field into a long-form argument with deep rough.

KeithK75 owned the net race at Shinnecock Hills, winning at +1 and clearing HamSammich by eight shots. MashedBROtato was another six back at +15, while Dan94 and DREADFUL tied the net number at +27. The raw scoreboard was even less competitive: Keith also won gross at -3, backed by 17.79 total strokes gained, including 8.05 on approach and 6.68 around the greens. Dan94 was the gross runner-up at +19, but the net math put him fourth. This was Keith’s cleanest kind of beatdown: four birdies, no bogeys, 10 greens, 24 putts, and not a single three-putt.
