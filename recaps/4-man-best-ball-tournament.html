KeithK75 NET +1 · GROSS -3

Approach play with a side order of violence

KeithK75 delivered the kind of round that makes a net leaderboard feel like a formality. He won net at +1 and gross at -3, with the raw golf doing almost all the yelling: 8.05 SGT SG on approach and 6.68 around the greens created a 15.35 tee-to-green total. Four birdies, zero bogeys, and 24 putts made this look less like survival and more like an organized mugging of Shinnecock. The lone blemish was a par-4 seven where one green-side adventure became another, but he still holed an 11-footer to stop the bleeding. Across seven prior starts, his average gross finish was 1.57, and this round fits the established profile: Keith’s ball-striking keeps showing up with paperwork.

2

HamSammich NET +9 · GROSS +37

Driving hard, approaching somewhere else

HamSammich secured the net runner-up spot at +9 despite a gross round of +37, which tells you exactly where the useful golf was hiding. The driver was serviceable and the putter provided a 2.37 SGT lift, but the approach game gave back 2.06 and tee-to-green finished negative. Four greens in regulation turned every hole into a scramble, and seven bogeys plus four doubles made the card look like it had been edited by a committee. The signature disaster was a par-4 where repeated rough escapes eventually produced a net six. Still, the net result was solid relative to the raw mess, and his five-start profile continues to show the same split: net contention can survive even when the gross game is wearing a fake mustache.

3

MashedBROtato NET +15 · GROSS +39

Big drives, small relationship with fairways

MashedBROtato’s round had enough useful pieces to tease a much better score. The putting was almost perfectly neutral, so this was not a putter crime; it was more a case of powerful transportation followed by too many unscheduled layovers. A par-3 seven featured the full rough-to-green pilgrimage. After seven prior starts, the profile remains volatile in score but clear in shape: the ceiling is real when the approach play behaves.
