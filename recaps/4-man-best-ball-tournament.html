1

KeithK75 NET +1 · GROSS -3

Approach play with a side of surgical cleanup

This was a wire-to-wire net hammering built on real golf, not scoreboard smoke. KeithK75 finished +1 in net and -3 gross, with 17.79 total strokes gained led by 8.05 on approach and 6.68 in the short game. He hit 10 greens, made four birdies, and gave the field no bogeys to exploit. The lone blemish was a seven on the par-four third, where a trip from green-side promise back into deep rough briefly threatened to become performance art. Seven prior starts already showed a strong profile, but this round was a louder statement: his approach SG was well above his 5.64 average, and the putter added 2.44 without a single three-putt.

2

HamSammich NET +9 · GROSS +37

The putter kept filing appeals for the irons

HamSammich’s +9 net finish was held together by a putter that refused to let the round fully collapse. He gained 2.37 putting strokes and made 97 feet of putts, but the ball-striking story was grim: just four greens, -2.06 approach SG, and -1.58 tee-to-green. The par-four ninth captured the whole operation, with repeated deep-rough recovery attempts turning a manageable hole into a seven. Still, this was a meaningful step from his prior profile, where total SG averaged -2.19 per appearance; the current 0.79 was hardly pretty, but it was functional enough to keep the net chase alive.

3

MashedBROtato NET +15 · GROSS +39

Big drives, small margins, large amounts of rough

MashedBROtato produced a useful 4.79 total strokes gained, but the scorecard kept charging interest. After seven prior starts averaging -0.06 total SG, this was a clear raw-golf improvement, even if the net result still settled at +15.

4
