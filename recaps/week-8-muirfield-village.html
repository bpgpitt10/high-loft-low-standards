KeithK75 NET -2 · GROSS -5

The putter brought a wrench; the rest of the bag brought a receipt.

KeithK75 handled the net race like the only adult in the room and still took gross too, because why not collect both trophies while the field is busy donating strokes to Muirfield. The big story is the iron game: 17 greens, a gaudy tee-to-green number, and approach play that absolutely steamrolled the SGT benchmark. This wasn’t one of those cute little scrape-and-hope rounds either; he made six birdies, only one bogey, and even the “worst hole” was just a single bogey on 14. After 15 starts, this is becoming a pretty grossly consistent profile: strong net results, monster approach play, and enough putter support to keep the chaos out of the headlines. He’s basically a lawnmower with a scorecard.},{

IUDoc92 NET +1 · GROSS +10

The driver showed up late, but the irons and putter kept the funeral from getting worse.

IUDoc92 turned in one of those rounds where the tee game looked like it had been swallowed by a shark, yet the card somehow stayed alive because the irons and putter dragged the body back to shore. He was awful off the tee against the SGT benchmark, but the approach play was loud enough to matter and the putter chipped in enough to keep him in the net hunt. The scorecard tells the same story: plenty of fairways, a useful burst of greens, two birdies, and then the ugly little streak on 16, 17, and 10 that kept the round from ever getting comfortable. With 15 prior starts, this is still a volatile player, but the current shape is clear: when the approach game shows up, he can survive the rest of the nonsense.},{

3

MattF NET +2 · GROSS +2

The irons were meh, but the rest of the bag did enough emergency surgery.

MattF didn’t exactly stripe his way through Muirfield, but the round held together because the tee game, short game, and putting all did the heavy lifting while the irons wandered off to think about their choices. That combination is why he could stay near the top even with only seven greens and a few annoying one-over blips on the card. The funny part is that this looks a lot like his broader profile: enough complete-round competence to contend, not quite the iron dominance his best stuff usually hints at. Through seven starts, he’s been a steady player who can win ugly or lose pretty, and this one leaned firmly toward the former. Not glamorous, but it got the job done without any full-blown faceplant holes.

4

Bombs off the tee, then a cold finish from the rest of the bag.

Dan94 blasted the ball and then spent the rest of the round arguing with the scoring gods. That’s the familiar Dan94 tension: enough firepower to create winning shots, enough volatility in the scoring clubs to leave a couple of extra dents on the card. He still posted a useful round, with one-hole damage staying pretty minor, but the separation between gross and net was mostly the rest of the field cashing in more efficiently. Fifteen starts in, the profile is still loud driver, mixed bag elsewhere, which is a fun way to live if you enjoy unnecessary stress.},{
