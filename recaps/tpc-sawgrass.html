Tournament in 30 seconds

Keith left very little room for accounting.

Net determines the tournament. Gross and SGT strokes gained explain the golf underneath it.

Sawgrass belonged to Keith. He won net at -3, Dan was second at +1, CRANK third at +2, and graycrum fourth at +3. Unlike Bay Hill, there was no three-way handicap knife fight at the top; Keith simply made everybody chase a number they could not reach.

And the raw golf backed it up. Keith shot -3 gross with +18.13 Total SG, driven by a ridiculous +9.01 approach, 11 GIR and just 23 putts. Dan was second gross at +2 with +13.13 Total SG. The gap after those two was not subtle.
