Tournament in 30 seconds

CRANK takes Bay Hill.

Net determines the tournament. Gross and SGT strokes gained explain the golf underneath it.

Bay Hill came down to CRANK and TEV_11. CRANK posted -7 net, TEV_11 was next at -7, MashedBROtato took third at -7, and KeithK75 was fourth at -5. The top three all landed on the same number, so the official leaderboard order gets the final word.

Underneath that, KeithK75 played the best raw golf at -5 gross, with Dan94 next at -2. Keith finished at +11.46 Total SG, with 14 GIR and 25 putts (+2.21 tee, +4.76 approach). So the day had two truths: CRANK won the tournament, while Keith won the raw-golf argument.

Player by player

Net order. Gross context.

Roast first. Then enough data to explain what actually happened.

1

CRANK NET -7 · GROSS +5

Twenty-four putts: either elite touch or a heroic refusal to hit greens from far away.

CRANK posted -7 net from +5 gross off a 21 handicap. The cleanest part of the round was putting at +2.83 SG; the bill came due off the tee at -2.66. Five GIR, 24 putts, two birdies, and the triple on 8. It was exactly the sort of round the handicap format was invented to make dangerous.

2

TEV_11 NET -7 · GROSS +3

Twelve greens and 33 putts. The irons did their job and then waited several business days for the putter.

TEV gained +3.83 off the tee, hit 12 GIR and made five birdies. Approach was slightly negative and 33 putts kept a very clean ball-striking day from becoming something silly. His worst hole was only bogey, which is deeply antisocial behavior in this league.

3

MashedBROtato NET -7 · GROSS +1

Five birdies, two doubles. Mashed found both ends of the emotional spectrum and refused the middle.

Mashed was legitimately good: +7.24 Total SG, +2.45 approach, 26 putts and five birdies. The two doubles kept the card from getting absurd, but +1 gross off a 9 handicap was plenty to land on -7 net.

4

KeithK75 NET -5 · GROSS -5

Ten birdies and one triple. Apparently moderation was not available.

Keith hit 14 greens, gained +4.76 approach and finished at +11.46 Total SG. The triple on 3 was a genuine crater, but ten birdies and 25 putts made the rest of Bay Hill look suspiciously easy. Best gross round by three.

5

DREADFUL NET -4 · GROSS +13

The short game kept trying to save the round. The approaches kept creating new emergencies.

DREADFUL hit 10 greens but lost -3.68 on approach overall, while the short game clawed back +1.59. The seven on the par-3 14th was the headline disaster. A 17 handicap still turned +13 gross into a useful -4 net.

6

Dan94 NET -3 · GROSS -2

The irons were throwing darts. The putter spent the afternoon returning them.

Dan gained +4.88 tee and +5.32 approach, hit 12 GIR and still lost -2.57 putting. That's how a round with +9.46 Total SG ends at merely -2 gross. The ball-striking was violent; the putter was a tax.

7

graycrum NET -3 · GROSS +4

The putter quietly did excellent work while the rest of the bag tried not to attract attention.

graycrum gained +2.06 putting and +1.28 tee, with approach the only real leak at -0.90. Eight GIR, 29 putts and one triple on 10. Nothing flashy, just enough useful golf to get to -3 net.

8

IUDoc92 NET E · GROSS +18

Thirty-eight putts is less a putting stat than a long-term relationship with every green.

IU hit eight greens but needed 38 putts and lost strokes in every SG category, including -4.43 approach. The triple on 4 and four doubles did the rest. The 18 handicap dragged the card back to even net, but there was no hidden statistical case that this round was secretly fine.

9

HamSammich NET +1 · GROSS +13

A ten on a par 4 with missing shots is not a blow-up hole. It is a classified document.

Ham's approach play was actually good at +2.65, but the short game lost -4.80 and hole 15 detonated for a ten. Nine GIR and 31 putts were workable; one six-over hole was not. The round was basically fine if you delete the federal investigation.

10

Leroy4522 NET +3 · GROSS +15

Concrete on the tee shot, ten on the card. Bay Hill briefly became municipal infrastructure.

Leroy lost -4.68 approach and -2.34 off the tee, with eight GIR and 34 putts. Hole 8 supplied the ten, complete with concrete and omitted shot numbers. Short game was the only SG category above water, and it had far too much work.
