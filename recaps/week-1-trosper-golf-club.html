Tournament in 30 seconds

KeithK75 opened Week 1 at Trosper by taking the net trophy at -5, edging Dan94 by one and MattF by two. Dan stayed attached with strong tee-to-green work and a hot putter, while Matt’s putting kept him in the picture despite nearly no approach edge. Behind them, the leaderboard turned into a simulator-yard-sale: tnboles77 somehow posted even net while losing 5.26 SGT strokes, and Leroy4522’s seven-over ninth was less a hole than a hostage situation.

Player by player

Roast first. Then enough data to explain what actually happened.

1

KeithK75 NET -5 · GROSS -7

The irons showed up and filed a hostile takeover.

KeithK75 produced the round everyone else was trying to describe: net victory at -5, gross victory at -7, and enough birdie pressure to make the leaderboard feel pre-decided. The real engine was the approach play, backed by 15 greens and SGT approach SG of 6.53, while the putter contributed instead of demanding witness protection. Even the worst hole was only a bogey after a rough tee shot and a long recovery putt. Across eight prior starts, Keith had already built a strong raw-golf profile, and this round confirmed the shape of it: tee-to-green quality is the foundation, with the net results finally matching the underlying violence.

2

Dan94 NET -4 · GROSS -5

A tidy ball-striker with a putter full of alibis.

Dan94 was the only real threat to Keith, finishing one shot back in net and two back in gross after a compact, low-drama round. He hit 14 greens, avoided a three-putt, and got his best SGT signal from the putter, which kept the birdies coming even when the approach game was merely useful rather than explosive. His bogey on the ninth was a controlled escape: rough, a couple of tidy recovery shots, then a six-footer buried. The eight-start history says the tee-to-green game is usually the headline; here, the putter stole the paragraph and nearly stole the event.

3

MattF NET -3 · GROSS -3

The putter carried the irons like an exhausted caddie.

MattF’s first recorded start is already a pretty persuasive introduction: net -3 and gross -3, good for a clear third while making the approach game contribute almost nothing in SGT terms. The rescue operation came from the greens, where 4.2 putting SG helped turn seven birdies into a real score instead of a collection of near-misses. His worst hole, a double on the 17th, began with a miss that forced two recovery pitches before the cleanup work. There is no prior history to compare, but the opening identity is obvious: the putter was excellent, the short game was useful, and the irons are currently taking questions.

4

tnboles77 NET E · GROSS +11

The short game kept bailing out a tee-to-green fire.

tnboles77 finished even on the net board despite gross golf that wandered all the way to +11, a split-screen round if there ever was one. SGT tee and approach numbers were both underwater, and only five greens meant the short game spent the afternoon doing unpaid overtime. With no prior starts in the profile, this is a first-round snapshot, and the snapshot says the scrambling can survive chaos, but it cannot keep being asked to create it.

9

State of the league

What this one changes.

Week 1 confirms KeithK75 as the early benchmark: his net win matched the strongest raw round, not a lucky leaderboard escape. Dan94 remains the closest challenger, while the middle of the league is wide open; tnboles77’s net result and IUDoc92’s history both show how much the net board can diverge from gross golf. The bigger question is whether Keith’s approach-driven edge holds when the field stops donating recovery shots.
