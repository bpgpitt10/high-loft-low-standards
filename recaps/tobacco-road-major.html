Tournament in 30 seconds

Mashed won both tournaments at once.

Net determines the tournament. Gross and SGT strokes gained explain the golf underneath it.

Mashed won the actual event at -4 net, five clear of CRANK at +1. DREADFUL took third at +4 and Dan was fourth at +8. There was no need to explain away the handicap result: Mashed also posted the best raw score in the field.

His +6 gross came with +13.75 Total SG, including +6.14 approach, +2.55 short game and +2.93 putting. Dan was second gross at +8 with +11.75 Total SG. Keith, normally allergic to the middle of the board, shot +12. Tobacco Road managed to make almost everyone look ordinary except Mashed, which is frankly the least ordinary possible outcome.

Player by player

Net order. Gross context.

Roast first. Then enough data to explain what actually happened.

1

MashedBROtato NET -4 · GROSS +6

An eagle, two birdies and three doubles. Mashed refuses to play a round with only one genre.

Mashed gained +6.14 approach, +2.55 short game and +2.93 putting for +13.75 Total SG. Six GIR and 29 putts do not scream domination, but the good holes were good enough and the disasters never got worse than double. He won both net and gross, which is the cleanest possible way to silence handicap complaints.

2

CRANK NET +1 · GROSS +16

Two greens in regulation and +6.99 short game. CRANK built the whole round out of emergency exits.

CRANK lost -4.56 approach but gained a huge +6.99 short game and +2.29 putting. Only two GIR, 25 putts, and somehow +3.75 Total SG. The nine on 4 was ugly, but a 15 handicap kept the round close enough to matter.

3

DREADFUL NET +4 · GROSS +22

The long game showed up. The short game responded by digging a bunker with a bunker inside it.

DREADFUL gained +2.28 tee and +4.33 approach, then lost -6.01 short game and -2.85 putting. Six GIR were not the issue. Hole 11's ten, with an extended bunker residency, was the round in miniature.

Hit the green in two, made eight. Somewhere a golf instructor just felt a disturbance in the Force.

Dan gained +5.27 tee and +5.97 approach with nine GIR and 29 putts. The +11.75 Total SG says the golf was strong; the eight on 16 says scores do not care about your underlying metrics.

04
State of the league

Mashed took the major and the raw-golf receipt.

Tobacco Road was Mashed's complete chaos ceiling made useful: enough birdies and ball-striking to win, enough doubles to keep the experience unmistakably Mashed. CRANK again showed how little GIR matters when the short game goes fully feral, while Dan's underlying golf was better than the score suggested.
