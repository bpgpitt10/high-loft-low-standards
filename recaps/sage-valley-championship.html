Player by player

Net order. Gross context.

Roast first. Then enough data to explain what actually happened.

1

IUDoc92 NET -7 · GROSS +10

Net wizard, raw-golf arsonist

This was a net win built on survival, not pretty golf. IUDoc92 finished -7 while posting +10 gross, with the approach play doing the heavy lifting at +2.35 and the putter adding +1.01. The rest of the round was a scrapyard: -2.73 off the tee, -0.99 around the green, seven bogeys, and two doubles. Even the ugly holes kept producing net value, including a par-four escape on 17 that required 39 feet of putting before the six-footer finally dropped. After six prior starts averaging 6.17 in net finishes and losing 10.81 strokes per appearance, this is a massive swing in outcome and raw efficiency. The golf was not elegant, but the net scoreboard has no dress code.

2

MashedBROtato NET -2 · GROSS +10

Power golf with a frequent-flyer membership in trouble

MashedBROtato’s -2 net round had real ball-striking underneath the nonsense: nine greens, a 250 average drive on fairways, and 0.65 total strokes gained. The fairways hit number was only four, because apparently every drive needed a dramatic subplot, but the approach game still gained 1.21. The closing hole captured the whole day: deep rough, sand, a late fairway sighting, and a seven that somehow remained useful in net. Two birdies and a net eagle on the par-five fourth kept the damage contained. After a mixed stretch that included a net win and several negative SG rounds, this looked more like the capable version returning than a miracle pulled from the simulator’s lost-and-found.

3

HamSammich NET -2 · GROSS +13

Good short game, hostile putting department

HamSammich got to -2 net while the raw round sat at +13, a result driven by competent recovery work rather than a clean strike. That ugly hole was balanced by a net eagle on the eighth and another on the 16th. With four prior starts averaging 5.0 in net finishes and a recent net runner-up showing, this continues a fair trend of finding ways to score even when the gross card is actively objecting.

4

Dan94 NET -2 · GROSS -2

Elite tee-to-green golf, one stubborn distance-control leak

Dan94 played the best raw golf in the building, firing -2 gross with 12 greens, six birdies, and 11.65 strokes gained. The net board gave him -2 and fourth, but that does not change the underlying story: this was the cleanest round of the event. Across six prior starts, he has averaged a 1.83 gross finish and gained 12.02 strokes per appearance, so the gross win is confirmation, not a surprise. The net trophy simply went elsewhere this time.
