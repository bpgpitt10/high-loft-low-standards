KeithK75 GROSS -1 · NET +2

The irons showed up with a fake ID.

This was a scratch winner built almost entirely through approach play. Keith hit 12 greens and gained 4.51 strokes on approach, turning a course designed to make every miss feel personal into a controlled gross round at -1. The putter added 3.24, including birdie conversions, while the tee game contributed exactly nothing—which is fine when the irons are carrying the whole operation like an overworked caddie. Even the worst hole was just a four, though three putts from 11 feet kept the round from becoming completely unreasonable. Across seven prior starts, Keith’s profile already showed strong gross form and four gross wins; this was another very clean confirmation.

2

Dan94 GROSS E · NET E

A putting clinic with occasional wilderness excursions.

Dan’s even-par gross round was a proper challenger performance, powered by 4.42 strokes gained putting and enough approach quality to keep the card alive. The round’s identity was obvious: he could make the putts, but the tee-to-green route occasionally wandered into concrete, rough, and general municipal-property concerns. The par-three finish became a five after a poor opening position and a recovery that still left a 14-footer, but he limited the broader damage to two bogeys and one double. Seven prior starts show a strong gross profile with two gross wins, and this round kept that raw-golf reputation intact even without the scratch trophy.

4
