Player by player

Net order. Gross context.

Roast first. Then enough data to explain what actually happened.

1

CRANK NET -4 · GROSS +11

The short game carried a tee-box crime scene.

CRANK’s net win was built on damage control, not pretty golf. He finished -4 in net while posting +11 gross, with just 2 greens in regulation and a nasty -2.28 off the tee. That sounds like a round held together with duct tape, except the approach game gained 1.74 and the short game gained 2.81, enough to keep the wreckage pointed toward the trophy. The par-four mess late in the card still produced a net six, but zero three-putts and 24 total putts kept it from becoming a full collapse. With two net wins in his four prior starts and an average net finish of 1.75, this is now looking less like a fluke and more like a specialty.

2

KeithK75 NET -2 · GROSS -2

The gross machine briefly discovered the weeds.

KeithK75 played the best raw round by a mile and still had to settle for second in the net race. His -2 gross came with 15.02 total strokes gained, including 5.10 off the tee, 6.17 on approach, nine greens in regulation, and four birdies. That is not simulator golf so much as a hostile takeover. The lone blemish was a messy par-four recovery that required a five-footer to finish the job, but it stayed a bogey and never became a round-killer. His prior average gross finish was 1.75 with two gross wins across four starts, so the ball-striking profile remains exactly as advertised; this time, the net board simply had a different sense of humor.

3

IUDoc92 NET E · GROSS +19

Concrete, pinestraw, and four doubles walked into a scorecard.

IUDoc92’s even net finish was a genuine escape from the usual arithmetic, even though the gross card landed at +19 and total strokes gained sat at -5.98. Three greens in regulation and four double bogeys made this a survival round, with the tee game losing 3.46 and the short game another 1.98. The defining par-five disaster began on concrete and kept demanding new forms of punishment before a pair of long putts finally ended it. Still, the net result was a major jump from his prior average net finish of 7.75 across four starts. The golf remains volatile, but this was the first time in this group of events that the chaos actually climbed onto the podium.

4

Dan94 NET +2 · GROSS +2

The tee game was elite until the last hole filed a complaint.

Dan94 produced the second-best gross round at +2 and backed it with 11.02 total strokes gained, driven by 6.08 off the tee and 12 fairways found. Eight greens in regulation and only one bogey made the round feel controlled right up until the finish, when a par four became a +3 speed bump. The putting was basically neutral at 0.60, so this was not a magic-carpet performance; it was solid ball-striking with one ugly closing bill. His four-start history shows an average gross finish of 1.75 and average net finish of 4.0, and this round landed right in that neighborhood. The raw game remains reliably dangerous, even when the net scoreboard refuses to reward it.

5

DREADFUL NET +4 · GROSS +22

Zero greens, eight doubles, and somehow still plenty of putting.

DREADFUL’s +4 net result was a small miracle wrapped around a +22 gross round. He hit zero greens, lost 8.98 strokes overall, and gave away 4.18 with the putter, while eight double bogeys turned the card into a neighborhood watch alert. The short game never found a stable address either, losing 1.79, and the worst-hole mess featured a trip to the sand followed by more wandering before a five-footer finally ended it. His prior total strokes gained average was already -4.88 over four starts, so this was not a new genre of pain; it was a louder version. The net finish stayed respectable, but the raw golf was fighting every department at once.

6

Leroy4522 NET +10 · GROSS +22

The approach game left the building without saying goodbye.

Leroy4522’s +10 net finish came from a round that was mostly lost between the tee shot and the green. He managed seven fairways, but only three greens, and the approach game dropped 6.58 strokes while total strokes gained finished at -8.98. The putter was nearly innocent at 0.03 lost, which is important because the card did not need another suspect. The closing par four became a nine after repeated recovery attempts failed to produce an actual recovery, capping a day with six bogeys and one double. His prior average net finish was 6.5 across four starts, so the placement was not wildly outside his established range; the +10 score simply made the route there impossible to ignore.

7

MashedBROtato NET +14 · GROSS +24

The putter brought flowers to an approach-game funeral.

MashedBROtato had the strangest split in the field: a respectable 2.46 gained with the putter buried under -9.66 on approach. Three greens in regulation and -12.43 tee to green explain the +24 gross and +14 net far better than the two birdies do. The signature wreck came on a par five where the recovery shots kept relocating the problem instead of solving it, followed by a 19-footer that at least let the hole leave with dignity. This was a sharp reversal from the prior event, where he won both net and gross with 13.75 total strokes gained. Four-start history still shows a 5.0 average net finish, but the current round was a reminder that the approach game can erase a lot of otherwise competent golf very quickly.
