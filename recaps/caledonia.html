Tournament in 30 seconds

CRANK won the tournament. Keith won the raw-golf argument.

Net determines the tournament. Gross and SGT strokes gained explain the golf underneath it.

CRANK took Caledonia at -4 net, two clear of Keith at -2. IU was third at even, Dan fourth at +2, and DREADFUL fifth at +4. It was a proper handicap race: CRANK had to post a number, Keith had to chase without any cushion, and the middle of the board stayed relevant.

On raw golf, Keith was excellent: -2 gross, +15.02 Total SG, +5.10 tee, +6.17 approach, nine GIR and only 24 putts. Dan was next at +2 gross with +11.02 Total SG and +6.08 tee. CRANK's route was completely different: only two GIR, +2.81 short game, 24 putts, and enough cleanup to turn +11 gross into the winning number.
