CRANK NET -7 · GROSS +11

The tee shot was a hate letter; the rest was excellent customer service.

CRANK won the net race at -7 by turning ugly tee play into a complete scoring round. The driver cost him 5.1 strokes, but the approach game gave back 3.76, short game added 1.26, and the putter delivered 4.23. Ten greens and three birdies kept the machine moving, even with four three-putts and three double bogeys lurking in the basement. The defining mess was a seven on the par-four troublemaker late in the round, but he still made enough from 169 feet of putts to erase plenty of damage. With one prior net win across two earlier starts and a previous total SG average of -3.21, this is a possible change worth watching.

After a ninth-place net result in his only prior appearance, this was a sharp round of possible change.
