Tournament in 30 seconds

CRANK won the spreadsheet. Dan attacked the property.

Net determines the tournament. Gross and SGT strokes gained explain the golf underneath it.

CRANK won Alpine at -7 net, two clear of Ham at -5. Leroy and Dan both finished -3 net, with Leroy third on the official board. DREADFUL was next at +1. This was the handicap format doing exactly what it is supposed to do: four very different gross rounds all showing up in the same actual competition.

Raw golf was Dan by a mile. He shot -3 gross with +18.15 Total SG, including an absurd +7.91 tee and +7.86 short game. He hit 13 GIR, averaged 324 yards on fairways hit, and produced the season's 373-yard missile. Keith was second gross at +6. The tournament belonged to CRANK; the golf course itself probably remembers Dan.
